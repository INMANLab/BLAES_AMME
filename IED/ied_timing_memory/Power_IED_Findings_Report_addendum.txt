Power_IED_Findings_Report Addendum
Hippocampal Restriction Check for During-Stim IED Trials

Question
Were the during-stim IED trials effectively restricted to hippocampal regions only?

Answer
No. During-stim IEDs were hippocampal-dominant, but they were not restricted to
hippocampal regions only.

Dataset and rule
Raw encoding IED detections were taken from:
IED/AMMEBLAES_IEDs_trial_level_dissertation_study_usethis_cleaned_with_memory.csv

The during-stim window was defined as rows with DuringStim == Y.
Hippocampal involvement was defined from the raw Region label as either:
- Hippocampus
- Amygdala, Hippocampus
No separate CA or DG labels appeared among during-stim detections in this raw sheet.

Detection-level result
There were 445 during-stim IED detections across 24 patients and 224 unique trials.
Of these, 313 / 445 (70.3%) involved a hippocampal region, whereas 132 / 445
(29.7%) were non-hippocampal. This hippocampal proportion was significantly
higher than the non-hippocampal proportion (exact binomial p < .001).

Unique trial-level result
Across the 224 unique patient-trials with at least one during-stim IED:
- 150 / 224 (67.0%) were hippocampal-only
- 20 / 224 (8.9%) were mixed hippocampal + non-hippocampal
- 54 / 224 (24.1%) were non-hippocampal-only

Thus, 170 / 224 trials (75.9%) included any hippocampal during-stim IED, but the
set was not hippocampal-only overall. Hippocampal-only trials were significantly
more common than non-hippocampal-only trials (exact binomial p < .001).

Interpretation
The during-stim timing window in the power/IED report is not limited to
hippocampal events, but the sample is strongly weighted toward hippocampal
involvement. That pattern is consistent with a hippocampal-dominant receiver
interference account, while still leaving a meaningful minority of during-stim
IED trials in non-hippocampal regions.
